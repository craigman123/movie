Got questions?
FREQUENTLY ASKED
How do I book a ticket? ▼
Browse movies from the dashboard, choose a screening from the Schedule page, and confirm your booking. You'll receive a unique QR code and hex ID to your account instantly.
Can tickets sell out? ▼
Yes — LUMA tracks ticket availability in real time. If a screening reaches capacity, it will show as unavailable to prevent overselling. Book early to secure your spot!
What is the hex code on my ticket? ▼
Every ticket is assigned a cryptographically unique hexadecimal identifier alongside a QR code. This dual-layer system ensures no two tickets are ever the same and makes counterfeiting virtually impossible.
Which venues does LUMA support? ▼
Currently LUMA serves Gaisano Grand Main and Minglanilla in Cebu, Philippines, with Standard and Premium screening experiences at each location.
